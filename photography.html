Photography

I have been an avid photographer all my life. At first, I would take photos with consumer digital cameras I received as birthday presents. Since then, I have branched out to film (developing and scanning myself), DSLRs, and even back to vintage digital cameras. I am also an experienced concert photographer and love to do freelance work as well.
