One of my dreams is to build my own recording studio, something I explored in depth in ART458: Branding Design. I named the studio "Wires and Chords" after the last track on The Microphones' debut Album, "Tests," and dedicated it to cheap and accessible analog recording. Later on in the class, I also redesigned a local restaurant's logo to better reflect their values and experience.

Publication Design

Over my college experience, I gathered a lot of knowledge about publications through EMMIE Music Magazine and my graphic design education. Through ART546: Publication Design, I was able to create my own publications using InDesign, supplemented with designs I made in Photoshop and Illustrator.

Promotional Flyers

As a graphic designer, one of my most common responsibilities is creating promotional flyers. These flyers are important because they communicate information quickly and efficiently while still being visually appealing. I find that by carefully matching the design of the flyer to its message, this message becomes even more legible.
