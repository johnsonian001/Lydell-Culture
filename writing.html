Music Journalism

I have been an active member of EMMIE music magazine and WSUM 91.7 since my sophomore year. Since then, I've served as the Local Interest Editor and Promotions Director on EMMIE's Editorial Board and hosted two of my own radio shows at WSUM. My time in music journalism has trained me not only to be a good writer but also how to create visually appealing art/photos and use them in conjunction with writing to create effective magazine layouts. As the Promotions Director, I spearheaded an effort to create monthly music zines for the local music population in Madison.
